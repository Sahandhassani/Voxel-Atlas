A few useful details

Axial is a horizontal section, coronal separates front and back, and sagittal separates left and right. Image orientation follows the file’s spatial transform. RAS coordinates are millimeters along right, anterior, and superior axes.

This version is an educational viewer, with no automated diagnosis. Saved library volumes require your signed-in account; local file opening remains on this device. DICOM series are not supported in this version. Large volumes depend on your device’s memory and WebGL2 support.
